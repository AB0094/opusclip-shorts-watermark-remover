    What this can and cannot do

    The badge is fully opaque, so the pixels underneath it are gone — this reconstructs that patch, it does not recover the original. Over flat walls and shadows it is invisible; over fine hair or text it can soften. The mask follows the exact shape of the logo and lettering (not a box), recognised per frame from the pixels, so it works on any clip, including the animated intro.

    Open source, MIT. Models: MI-GAN (Picsart Research, ICCV 2023) and LaMa (Samsung AI), via ONNX Runtime Web.
